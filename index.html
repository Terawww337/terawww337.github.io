What I make

What interests me

The detail you almost miss. A familiar room that feels different. A conversation that means something else when you remember it later. I want curiosity and unease to carry the player into the story.

> WRITENOR

WriteNor

“A day that will not end. A past that will not stay buried.”

WriteNor is a first-person psychological horror. Johnny’s present stands still while memories take him back a year, to the office and to Nori. Explore, read messages and piece together why those two realities are drawing closer. Some truths only surface when you are willing to remember.

> CREATIVE_APPROACH

My Approach

[01]

A familiar place, one wrong detail

A scene can be unsettling before anything happens. Small changes give the player a reason to look again.

[02]

Curiosity comes first

I want the player to keep going because they need to understand the place, the people and the memory behind it.

[03]

Let the player discover

A room, a message or a pause in a conversation can carry a story. I prefer details the player finds to answers delivered all at once.

[04]

Emotion leaves a trace

Horror stays with us when there is something human underneath it. Memory, absence and unfinished conversations are at the heart of WriteNor.

“Make the player want to remember — even when it hurts.”

— Terawww / Creative direction
